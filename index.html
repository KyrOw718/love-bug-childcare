Good questions

Frequently Asked

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes — we're a fully licensed facility with liability insurance, and all staff hold current first-aid certification and criminal record checks.

What's your child-to-caregiver ratio?

We keep ratios intentionally small: 3:1 for infants, 4:1 for toddlers, and 6:1 for preschoolers, so every child gets real one-on-one time.

How do you handle allergies and dietary needs?

Our kitchen is nut-aware, and every allergy is posted in the kitchen and classroom. You'll review and sign an allergy plan with us before your child's first day.

What if my child gets sick at daycare?

We call you right away and keep your little one comfortable in a quiet space. Our full sick-child policy is shared during registration.

Do you accept childcare subsidy?

Absolutely. We're a subsidy-friendly centre and will help you navigate the application so nothing gets lost in the paperwork.

Is there a waitlist?

Spaces are first-come, first-served by registration date. Submitting the form below puts your little bug on our list right away — no deposit required to hold a spot in line.
